Dulquer Salmaan

About

Dulquer Salmaan (popularly known as DQ or Kunjikka) is a highly acclaimed Indian actor, playback singer, film producer, and entrepreneur who predominantly works in Malayalam cinema while establishing a powerful pan-Indian presence across Tamil, Telugu, and Hindi films. He is the younger son of the legendary Malayalam superstar

Quick Facts

  • Born: July 28, 1983 (or 1986), in Kochi, Kerala, India
  • Family: Son of veteran Malayalam superstar Mammootty and Sulfath
  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Business Management from Purdue University, USA
  • Spouse: Married architect Amal Sufiya on December 22, 2011
